I'm an investigative journalist specializing in cybersecurity, data privacy, and technology policy.

## Background

With nearly two decades of journalism experience, I've covered some of the most significant developments in tech law, corporate security practices, and digital privacy rights. My reporting has appeared in leading legal and technology publications including Law.com, Courthouse News Service, and MLex.

## Coverage Areas

My reporting focuses on:
- **Cybersecurity & Data Breaches** - Corporate security failures, incident response, and regulatory enforcement
- **Privacy Law & Policy** - GDPR, CCPA, and emerging privacy regulations
- **Tech Litigation** - High-stakes cases involving technology companies and digital rights
- **Regulatory Enforcement** - SEC, FTC, and state attorney general actions related to data security

## Selected Work

My investigative work has shed light on corporate cybersecurity practices, executive accountability in data breach cases, and the evolving legal landscape surrounding digital privacy.
