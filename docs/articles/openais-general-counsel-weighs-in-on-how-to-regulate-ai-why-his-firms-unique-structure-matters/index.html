OpenAI's General Counsel Weighs In on How to Regulate AI, Why His Firm's Unique Structure Matters

Law.com October 16, 2023
Artificial Intelligence

OpenAI General Counsel Che Chang says he’s a strong proponent of artificial intelligence regulation, a view that puts him in line with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman but less so with other AI industry executives.

He said regulators are “trying to make sure humanity stays at the forefront of technology, not the other way around,” Chang said. “I think that’s what we all want.”
