Mechanic Injured on Conveyor Belt at FedEx Warehouse Files Negligence, Premises Liability Claim

Law.com November 16, 2023

An El Paso, Texas, mechanic filed a premises liability claim in New Mexico court earlier this month alleging that FedEx failed to properly train its employees or maintain proper security procedures when he was seriously injured while fixing a conveyor belt.

This suit was first surfaced by Law.com Radar.
