Fight Over Border Wall Funding Diversion Hashed Out in California

Courthouse News Service November 20, 2019
Environmental Law

OAKLAND, Calif. (CN) – In a federal courtroom in Oakland, California, on Wednesday, Trump’s Department of Justice clashed with lawyers for the Sierra Club, the state of California and a slew of other states looking to block the Pentagon from diverting $3.6 billion from the military to build a southern border wall.

Justice Department attorney James Burnham told U.S. District Judge Haywood Gilliam that proposed border barriers in California, New Mexico and Arizona are covered by 10 U.S. Code § 2808, which allows the Department of Defense, in the event of a national emergency requiring the armed forces, to spend military construction funds on projects necessary to support the armed forces.
