'Deeply Troubling': Tesla Sued Over 'Racially Hostile Work Environment' Amid Surge of Racial Discrimination Litigation

Law.com June 17, 2024
Employment LawTitle VII

Tesla was hit with another complaint alleging racial discrimination on Monday in Alameda County Superior Court just three months after settling a separate race harassment lawsuit.

The plaintiffs, represented by Arias Sanguinetti Wang & Team and Law Brothers – Injury Attorneys (named as Lalezary Law Firm in the complaint), include both current and former Tesla employees who worked at Tesla’s electric vehicle manufacturing facilities in Fremont and Lathrop, California. They allege that supervisors and colleagues at these factories created a “racially hostile work environment” by routinely harassing African American, Hispanic and Latinx Tesla employees.
