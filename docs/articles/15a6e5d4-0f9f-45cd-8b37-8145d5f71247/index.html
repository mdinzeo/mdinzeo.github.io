Distributor Hopes Bootleggers 'Can't Get No Satisfaction' Selling Unauthorized Rolling Stones Merch

Law.com February 1, 2024

Universal Music Group’s merchandising company filed a trademark infringement lawsuit in federal court Monday asking the court to enjoin bootleggers from selling unauthorized Rolling Stones merchandise outside a Houston stadium for the band’s upcoming concert.
