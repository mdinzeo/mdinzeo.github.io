'You Can’t Do a First Draft of Common Sense': Microsoft GC Jon Palmer Talks AI, Litigation, and Leadership

Law.com November 25, 2024
Artificial Intelligence

Jon Palmer didn’t expect to wind up the general counsel at Microsoft when he joined as senior attorney back in 2010. He hadn’t even expected to go in-house. But after decades as outside counsel the opportunity to work for his favorite client was too good to pass up.

“I was always super impressed by the litigators that I worked with there, how strategic they were, the role they played at the company and how thoughtful they were,” he said. “I loved being at law firms and I never really thought much about going in house, but because I knew the litigation group so well, it was really interesting and attractive. So that's why I made the jump.”
