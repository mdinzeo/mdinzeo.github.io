Jury urged to hold Meta 'accountable' for recording sensitive data as Flo trial wraps

MLex July 31, 2025

Flo period-tracking app urged a federal jury to tell Meta Platforms that "they don't get a pass" for secretly recording sensitive reproductive health data. Meta pushed back in its own closing arguments, saying it never recorded, only received, strings of code created by Flo. Millions of women have entered sensitive information about their menstrual cycles and reproductive health into a period-tracking app that they expected would be kept private. But Meta Platform’s software was always listening, said a lawyer for a class of women suing Meta for eavesdropping and recording their conversations with the Flo app. . . .
