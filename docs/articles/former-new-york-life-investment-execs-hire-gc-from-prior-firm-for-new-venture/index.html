Former New York Life Investment Execs Hire GC From Prior Firm for New Venture

Law.com April 10, 2023

Six former GoldPoint Partners executives who abruptly quit the private equity affiliate of New York Life Insurance Co. two years ago are launching a new investment firm and hiring away GoldPoint’s general counsel to be its legal chief.

The new firm, Dextra Partners, announced Monday that Lorne Smith, had ended his 17-year career with New York Life and will serve as general counsel and chief compliance officer of New York City-based Dextra.
