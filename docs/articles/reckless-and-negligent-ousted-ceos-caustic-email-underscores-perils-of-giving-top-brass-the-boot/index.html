'Reckless and Negligent': Ousted CEO's Caustic Email Underscores Perils of Giving Top Brass the Boot

Law.com February 21, 2024

Terminating a company’s CEO poses its own legal and operational risks. But having the ex-CEO stick around as a board member can be even more awkward, as Planet Fitness is finding out.

This week, the Hampton, New Hampshire-based chain of 2,400 fitness centers said Chris Rondeau had resigned from the board over disagreements with the way the company has been run since his surprise ouster as CEO in September.
