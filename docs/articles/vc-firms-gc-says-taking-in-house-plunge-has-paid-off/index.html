VC Firm's GC Says Taking In-House Plunge Has Paid Off

Law.com January 19, 2022

Livingston Miller always has had a passion for working with people and companies that are disrupting industries. After spending more than a decade as an attorney at two major law firms, he moved in-house, serving in roles he found rewarding.

Last year, the husband and father of three joined the San Francisco-based venture capital firm Initialized Capital as its first general counsel, overseeing both legal and compliance functions.
