Affeld England & Johnson Secures $138 Million Verdict for Bitcoin Miner in Contract Breach Suit

Law.com July 18, 2024
A Bitcoin miner represented by Affeld England & Johnson scored a $138 million verdict win in a breach-of-contract suit. The miner, Michael Ho, alleged that crypto mining company Marathon Digital Holdings breached a non-disclosure/non-circumvention agreement with him after he shared proprietary information about an energy producer with the company.
