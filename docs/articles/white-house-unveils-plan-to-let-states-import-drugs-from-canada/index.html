White House Unveils Plan to Let States Import Drugs From Canada

Courthouse News Service December 18, 2019
Healthcare Law

(CN) – The Trump administration is forging ahead with its plan to allow states to import prescription drugs from Canada – a move aimed at lowering prices for Americans, but one that’s drawing warnings from law enforcement and drug industry experts.

“For the first time in history, HHS and FDA are open to importation as a means to lower drug prices,” Secretary of Health and Human Services Alex Azar told reporters in a press call, referring to Health and Human Services and the Food and Drug Administration by their acronyms.
