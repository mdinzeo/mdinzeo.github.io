'I'm Aghast': Viral Paul Hastings Presentation Garners Strong In-House Reaction

Law.com April 7, 2023
Employment Law

A presentation by a senior Paul Hastings associate with a demanding list of “nonnegotiable expectations” for junior colleagues is sparking a broad range of reactions in the in-house community, from a shrug to outrage.

The debate reverberated across social media in recent days, with the outcry on Twitter especially spirited. “Definite big time Glengarry Glen Ross energy coming through on this one,” one commenter tweeted. “Reason 6,540,329 I am so glad I had nothing to do with these firms,” said another. Others ridiculed the firm’s $850-per-hour rack rate for a junior associate.
