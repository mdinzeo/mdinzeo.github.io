Lawyers Urge Uniformity in Courts’ Pandemic Response

Courthouse News Service February 22, 2021



(CN) – Legal aid attorneys urged California lawmakers on Tuesday Feb. 23 to impose uniformity on state courts trying to operate in the midst of a pandemic, favoring rules that promote technology to resolve cases over in-person hearings, and consistent health and safety guidelines for the state’s 58 trial courts.
