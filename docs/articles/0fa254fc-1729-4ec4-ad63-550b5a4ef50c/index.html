Retail Tech Startup Taps Farfetch Exec to Lead, Build Out Legal Team

Law.com July 24, 2024

Radar, a startup whose hardware and software tools help retailers track inventory, has hired Morgan Levine as its first general counsel.

Levine most recently spent five years at the online luxury fashion seller Farfetch, rising to vice president of legal by the time she departed last December. She joined the London-based company, which launched in 2007, as the first lawyer in its North American division and eventually built an international team of 15 in-house counsel.
