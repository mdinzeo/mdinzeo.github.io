Iron Mountain General Counsel Says CEOs Putting More Pressure on In-House Leaders to Drive Diversity

Law.com August 13, 2020
Employment Law

For the last several years, general counsels have been putting pressure on outside law firms to hire more diverse lawyers.

Now, CEOs are leaning on their in-house leaders to drive corporate diversity initiatives amid heightened public awareness and outrage over inequality and systemic racism, according to Deborah Marson, general counsel, executive vice president and secretary of Iron Mountain Inc.
