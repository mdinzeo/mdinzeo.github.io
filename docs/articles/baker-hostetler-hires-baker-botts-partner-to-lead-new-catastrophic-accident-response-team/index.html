Baker & Hostetler Hires Baker Botts Partner to Lead New Catastrophic Accident Response Team

Law.com April 22, 2024

Baker Botts partner Greg Dillard has joined Baker & Hostetler as a partner in Houston, where he will lead the firm’s newly created Catastrophic Accident Response team.

DIllard, who started at Baker & Hostetler on Monday, is a partner in the firm’s litigation practice group and a member of the energy industry team. He will also help lead the firm’s Workplace Safety OSHA practice team.
