Report: High Court Ruling in Abortion Case More About Deceptive Speech

Courthouse News Service January 24, 2019
Planned Parenthood v. DaleidenFree Speech

A 2018 U.S. Supreme Court ruling that struck down a California law requiring faith-based pregnancy centers to provide women with information about family planning and abortions has far-reaching consequences for government regulation, public health and commercial disclosures, according to an analysis published Thursday by the NYU College of Global Public Health.
