Comcast Legal Chief Breaks Into the $10M Pay Club

Law.com April 25, 2024

If NBCUniversal Pictures parent Comcast Corp. made a movie about highly paid lawyers, 2023 would be the year Thomas Reid debuted on the big screen.

Reid, Comcast’s chief legal officer and secretary, earned $11.2 million last year, according to the company’s proxy statement filed Friday with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
