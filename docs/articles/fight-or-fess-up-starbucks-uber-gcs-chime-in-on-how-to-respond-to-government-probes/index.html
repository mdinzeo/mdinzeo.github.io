Fight or Fess Up? Starbucks, Uber GCs Chime In on How to Respond to Government Probes

Law.com March 7, 2024

Choose your battles carefully. Credibility is key. And for god’s sake, don’t bore the board of directors. This was some of the plainspoken advice an all-star lineup of general counsel had for outside counsel on how to best represent companies in the investigatory hot seat.

Their remarks came Thursday at the American Bar Association’s white-collar crime conference in San Francisco, just hours after Deputy Attorney General Lisa Monaco announced a greater push to encourage people to report on companies by rewarding whistleblowing and incentivizing companies to disclose wrongdoing as early as possible.
