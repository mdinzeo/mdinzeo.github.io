Why Women Lawyers Must 'Get Rid of Their Inner Perfectionist'

Law.com October 18, 2023

Clinical psychologist Traci Cipriano has a few questions for women lawyers. When was the last time you took a vacation? When was the last time you had fun? When was the last time you got up from your desk and took a 15-minute walk?

Women are being pulled in more directions than ever—juggling work responsibilities with family life and the mental load of being the point person at home, as well as the pressure to do it all perfectly.
