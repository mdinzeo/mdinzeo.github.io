In Big Law Partner Hiring, Business Plans Become 'Litmus Test' For Government Hires

Law.com April 9, 2024

When searching for lateral partners to hire, law firms are putting much more weight on candidates’ business plans, particularly government attorneys who are transitioning to private practice, according to recruiters for firms.

Such business plans often incorporate a government candidate’s network and skills and how they expect to turn their expertise into revenue within the first years of practice, among other details.
