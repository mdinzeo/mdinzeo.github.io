Longtime Law Firm Partner Going In-House to Help Companies Control Legal Bills

Law.com February 27, 2024

After 30 years as a partner at some of the world’s most prominent law firms, Brian Arbetter is going in-house, leveraging decades of law firm experience to help corporate clients control their outside counsel spend as the first general counsel and vice president of law firm relations for LegalBillReview.com.

Arbetter got his start doing labor and employment work at Mitchell Silberberg & Knupp in the early 1990s. Since then, he’s been an equity partner at such prestigious firms as Baker McKenzie, Sheppard Mullin and Norton Rose Fulbright, where he specialized in international labor and employment law.
