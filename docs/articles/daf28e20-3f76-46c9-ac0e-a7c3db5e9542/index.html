Hiring From a Law Firm for an In-House Position? Use Caution

Law.com June 30, 2024

Within my niche practice of recruiting attorneys exclusively for companies, 90% of the lawyers my search firm has placed in the past four years came with current or previous in-house experience.  Our mix of placement backgrounds was closer to 60/40 just ten years ago, meaning almost half of our placements were lawyers moving into their first in-house position.

I think this is a meaningful observation that speaks to change that has occurred on a boarder scale.  Specifically, law firm career paths and in-house career paths are increasingly viewed as two divergent roads.  It used to be much easier for a law firm Partner to move in-house as, to stay with the metaphor, the transition was viewed more as just changing lanes.
