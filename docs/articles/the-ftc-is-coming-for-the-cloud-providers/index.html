The FTC Is Coming for the Cloud Providers

Law.com April 5, 2023
AntitrustCybersecurity

Cloud providers are the latest target of the Federal Trade Commission as it continues to put the screws to Big Tech on competition.

Through a request for information on tech companies’ business practices, the FTC is laying the groundwork for a future enforcement action against the cloud computing market’s biggest players, said Benjamin Rossen, a former senior attorney at the FTC’s privacy and identity protection division.
