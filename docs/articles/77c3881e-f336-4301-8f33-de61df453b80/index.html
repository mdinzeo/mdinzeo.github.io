Paramount General Counsel Heads for Exit in Wake of Failed Merger

Law.com June 19, 2024

Christa D’Alimonte is stepping down as general counsel of New York-based Paramount Global after more than a decade with the media conglomerate.

D’Alimonte broke the news to her team in an internal memo this week that was published by entertainment news sites Deadline and Variety. Her last day will be June 28. Deadline reported that she will be succeeded by 15-year Paramount veteran Caryn Groce, who’s been deputy general counsel for four years.
