Jailed Ex-Lawyer Presses Appeal for Janis Joplin, Gordon Lightfoot Royalties

Law.com February 22, 2024

The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit in New York on Thursday remanded a proceeding involving the estate of legendary music manager Albert Grossman, his late wife Sally Grossman, and the music royalties of Janis Joplin and Gordon Lightfoot, to a state surrogate’s court.

The panel of circuit judges Jose Cabranes, Robert Sack and Myrna Perez, meanwhile, dismissed a parallel action, on grounds that it is not reviewable on appeal.
