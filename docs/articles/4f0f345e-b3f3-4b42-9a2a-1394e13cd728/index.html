What Is Exhibit J? Litigators Are Watching This AI Test Case

Law.com March 11, 2024

Deep in the New York Times’ complaint against Microsoft Corp. and OpenAI, the poster child of artificial-intelligence startups, is Exhibit J, the crux of the copyright-infringement lawsuit that accuses the defendants of using the Times’ copyright-protected content to train its AI model GPT-4.

Attorneys tell ALM publication and Law.com affiliate Legaltech News that Exhibit J is the most striking part of the complaint, and likely what elevates this suit above its predecessors.
