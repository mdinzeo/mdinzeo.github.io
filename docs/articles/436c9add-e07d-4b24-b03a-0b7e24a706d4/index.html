Ransomware Gangs Are Increasingly Going After Smaller, Easier Targets

Law.com August 2, 2023

Small companies beware: You’re more likely to be a victim of a ransomware attack.

A new report on the 2023 ransomware threat landscape from cyber-risk quantification platform Kovrr found ransomware actors tend to favor companies with revenue in the $10 million to $50 million range.
