Longtime GC of Biotech That Discovered Leading Hep-C Cure Announces Retirement

Law.com December 21, 2023

The longtime general counsel of drug discovery firm Entanta Pharmaceuticals will retire at the end of March, the company announced in a securities filing made public this week.

Nathaniel Gardiner, 70, has been with Entanta since its founding in 1995. He was hired to lead its legal department in 2014, having served as its outside counsel while a partner at Boston law firm Edwards Wildman Palmer, which merged with Dallas-based Locke Lord LLP in 2015.
