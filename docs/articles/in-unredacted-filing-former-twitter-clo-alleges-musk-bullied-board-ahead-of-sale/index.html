In Unredacted Filing, Former Twitter CLO Alleges Musk Bullied Board Ahead of Sale

Law.com June 7, 2024
Elon MuskTwitter v. Musk

Incensed that he couldn’t back out of his agreement to buy Twitter, Elon Musk allegedly sought to fire every in-house lawyer who worked on the lawsuit to force him to close the deal and tried to intimidate the board of directors into lowering the purchase price, according to a newly unredacted complaint filed by lawyers representing its former legal chief, general counsel, CEO and CFO in a severance dispute.

The new filing follows a May 31 order by U.S. District Senior Judge Maxine Chesney of the Northern District of California to unseal portions of the complaint that Musk and Twitter, now known as X Corp., had tried to keep private. Chesney found the majority of the redacted information was not confidential.
