Greenberg Traurig Files Suit Accusing Groups of Acting as Hamas 'Propaganda Division,' Spreading Falsehoods

Law.com April 11, 2024

Attorneys with Greenberg Traurig, the National Jewish Advocacy Center, Schoen Law Firm, and Holtzman Vogel have filed a lawsuit on behalf of nine victims of the Oct. 7 Hamas attack on Israel, accusing particular groups for the recent propaganda division in the United States.

The lawsuit was filed Wednesday in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia against AJP Educational Foundation, also known as American Muslims for Palestine, a nonprofit corporation with its principal place of business in Falls Church, Virginia, and the National Students for Justice in Palestine (NSJP), an unincorporated association founded by AMP to provide on-campus management or control of hundreds of university chapters of Students for Justice in Palestine.
