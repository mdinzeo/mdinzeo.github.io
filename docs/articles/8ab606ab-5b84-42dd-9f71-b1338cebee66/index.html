Women's General Counsel Network Founder Joins Climate Consultancy as It Expands Globally

Law.com June 11, 2023

Fast-growing climate consultancy 3Degrees Group hired Jan Kang, a veteran in-house leader and founder of the Women’s General Counsel Network, as general counsel.

Kang was previously chief operating officer and general counsel at cybersecurity startup Stairwell, where she was also a board member. After leaving the GC role in October, she was an adviser until her official departure in January.
