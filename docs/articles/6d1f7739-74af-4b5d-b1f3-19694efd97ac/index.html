In-House Counsel Saw Pay Rise Over Past Year, Despite Drop in Job-Hopping

Law.com May 15, 2024

Compensation is creeping up again for general counsel and other senior in-house lawyers, according to a new in-house compensation report from executive search firm BarkerGilmore.

Median compensation for in-house counsel rose 4.4% more in 2024 compared with a year earlier, the report says, with managing counsel seeing the largest increase (3.8%), followed by general counsel (3.2%) and senior counsel (2.9%).
