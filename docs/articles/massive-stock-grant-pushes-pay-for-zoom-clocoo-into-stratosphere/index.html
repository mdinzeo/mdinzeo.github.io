Massive Stock Grant Pushes Pay for Zoom CLO/COO Into Stratosphere

Law.com May 8, 2023

The top lawyer at Zoom Video Communications, Aparna Bawa, who also serves as chief operating officer, saw her compensation rocket to $28 million in 2022, thanks to $27.6 million in stock grants, according to a newly filed proxy statement.

The company awards periodic grants to executives, rather than making them every year. A year earlier, Bawa did not receive any grants, leaving her total pay at just $419,940.
