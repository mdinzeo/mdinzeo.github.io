Veteran AMC Networks General Counsel to Retire at Year's End

Law.com July 29, 2024

AMC Networks’ general counsel Jamie Gallagher will retire at the end of 2024 after 16 years at the legal helm, the entertainment company announced in a regulatory filing.

Gallagher steered the network through a number of milestones, including its 2011 IPO, and the creation of AMC Studios, the network’s in-house production studio behind hits like Mad Men, Breaking Bad and The Walking Dead. Gallagher also helped establish its targeted streaming portfolio, led by its flagship streamer AMC+.
