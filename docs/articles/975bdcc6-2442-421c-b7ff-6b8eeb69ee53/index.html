Hard Rock Atlantic City Brings New General Counsel to the Table

Law.com February 25, 2021

Nanette Horner, vice president of legal and general counsel at Hard Rock Hotel & Casino Atlantic City. Courtesy photo

Former Empire Resorts Inc. chief lawyer Nanette Horner has checked into the Hard Rock Hotel & Casino as vice president of legal and general counsel. She has worked in the gaming industry since the mid-1990s as a private attorney, in-house leader and regulator.
