Aviation Startup Behind 'Flying Taxis' Lands First Legal Chief

Law.com May 16, 2023

Skyryse, an autonomous flight software startup that aims to make flying taxis a reality, has found its first chief legal officer in in-house veteran Blake Bilstad, who has a long history of shepherding companies through periods of rapid growth.

Bilstad joins Skyryse from fitness company Beachbody, where for the last 20 months he’s been chief legal officer as it extended its reach in multilevel marketing.
