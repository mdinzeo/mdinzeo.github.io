Tesla Legal Chief Aims to Build In-House Litigation Powerhouse

Law.com April 4, 2023

Tesla’s new legal chief is on the hunt for Big Law partners with litigation and trial experience to beef up its in-house team.

General counsel Brandon Ehrhart, who joined the company in January from DISH Network, put out a call for applicants on LinkedIn last week, writing, “Are you an experienced partner at a top law firm? Do you enjoy handling sophisticated litigation and trials but are frustrated by internal issues like conflicts and passing on work for clients you love? Tesla Legal is building something unique: a full-scale internal litigation and trial team that can handle all aspects of litigation and trial work, including briefings, hearings, discovery, depositions and trials, completely in-house.”
