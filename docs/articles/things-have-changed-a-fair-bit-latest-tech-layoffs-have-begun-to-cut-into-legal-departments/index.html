'Things Have Changed a Fair Bit': Latest Tech Layoffs Have Begun to Cut Into Legal Departments

Law.com February 23, 2023

Legal departments are staying mostly intact as layoffs continue to batter the tech industry, though recruiters say in-house teams at larger companies are starting to feel the pinch.

“A lot of GCs I’ve talked to have been able to hold onto their teams,” said Lina Guo, a managing director for Major, Lindsey & Africa’s in-house counsel recruiting team in San Francisco. “The really large tech companies have been pretty indiscriminate when it comes to layoffs, and there’s a certain percentage of folks getting cut at companies that have a lot of lawyers. Small to midsize companies have maybe lost a couple. But the legal teams weren’t necessarily bloated to begin with.”
