US tech industry challenge to Maryland's 'Kids Code' advances

MLex November 25, 2025

Act move forward, finding the tech trade group had plausibly raised First Amendment concerns and argued that the statute conflicts with COPPA and Section 230. A US federal judge has allowed NetChoice’s legal challenge to Maryland’s Age-Appropriate Design Code Act to advance, ruling Monday that the trade group plausibly alleged the law violates the First Amendment and is preempted by both the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act and Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act. . . .
