Seyfarth’s New Chief D&I Officer Aims to Maximize Benefits of Diverse Workforce

Law.com February 27, 2024

Seyfarth Shaw recently named a new chief diversity and inclusion officer, hiring Mira Dewji, the former DEI director at Fenwick & West, who started at Seyfarth’s New York office this week.

Dewji, who left Fenwick in 2022, said her hiring was the product of an extended search after Seyfarth’s first D&I leader, Kori Karew, departed the firm to launch her own consultancy at the end of 2022.
