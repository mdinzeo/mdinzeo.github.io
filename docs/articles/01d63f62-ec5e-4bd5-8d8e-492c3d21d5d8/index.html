Firms Are Stuck With Abiding by New Anti-Secrecy Law, Even Though Judge Ruled It Unconstitutional

Law.com April 15, 2024

Companies anticipating a reprieve from the Corporate Transparency Act’s reporting requirements may be celebrating prematurely.

Even though a federal judge in Alabama last month ruled that the anti-money-laundering law is unconstitutional, the U.S. Department of the Treasury said that it nonetheless will enforce the measure and expects organizations to file beneficial ownership information with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network by an end-of-year deadline.
