Judge rules BrandTotal’s data harvesting violates Meta’s terms of use and anti-hacking laws

Courthouse News Service June 6, 2022
PrivacyCFAAMeta

SAN FRANCISCO (CN) — Israeli ad consulting firm BrandTotal violated Facebook’s terms of use when it harvested user data without permission, a federal magistrate judge found in the latest round of wrangling ahead of a trial set for late summer over BrandTotal’s data scraping practices.

BrandTotal helps corporate clients interact with their ads and those of their competitors on social media through one of its most popular products, a Google Chrome browser extension called UpVoice that automatically collected data on ads that its paid “panelists” saw on the site.
