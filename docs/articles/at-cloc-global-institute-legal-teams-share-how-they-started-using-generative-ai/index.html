At CLOC Global Institute, Legal Teams Share How They Started Using Generative AI

Law.com May 6, 2024
Artificial Intelligence

No legal department wants to get left behind on generative AI. But corporate legal departments are in various stages of readiness to adopt this rapidly evolving technology, and legal leaders are still figuring out how to unlock its potential while mitigating risk.

During a panel discussion Tuesday at the CLOC Global Institute conference in Las Vegas, a diverse group of general counsel and legal operations leaders shared how they are making use of generative AI and how they got started.
