In First-Impression Case, Deeply Split Montana High Court Says Facebook Posts Created Minimum Contacts With State

Law.com October 22, 2023
Wildlife photographer Melissa Groo will have to face a suit in Montana over her negative Facebook posts about the Triple D Game Farm. The wildlife photography farm said Groo's posts interfered with its business and filed tort claims against her in Montana state court. Three dissenting judges disagreed from the majority and said Groo's posts were intended for a national audience, not just Montana residents. ...
