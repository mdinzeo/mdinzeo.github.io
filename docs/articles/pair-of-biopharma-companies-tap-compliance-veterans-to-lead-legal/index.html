Pair of Biopharma Companies Tap Compliance Veterans to Lead Legal

Law.com January 22, 2024
Healthcare Law

Two East Coast pharma companies are adding general counsel to their executive ranks, hiring compliance leaders with deep biotech industry experience to head their legal teams.

Tris Pharma—a New Jersey-based drugmaker focused on treatments for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, addiction and other neurological diseases—hired Franchesca Fowler as its chief compliance officer and general counsel.
