Family of Exonerated Black Man Killed by a Georgia Deputy Is Suing Him in Federal Court

Law.com February 25, 2024
Criminal Justice

The family of a Black man fatally shot by a Georgia deputy during an October traffic stop filed a federal lawsuit in the Southern District of Georgia on Tuesday, seeking more than $16 million in damages, arguing that the deputy used excessive force and that the sheriff who employed him ignored the officer’s history of violence.

Leonard Cure, 53, was killed just three years after Florida authorities freed him from a 16-year imprisonment for a crime he did not commit.
