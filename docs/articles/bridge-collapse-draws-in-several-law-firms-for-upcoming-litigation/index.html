Bridge Collapse Draws in Several Law Firms for Upcoming Litigation

Law.com April 16, 2024

Two large law firms as well as several plaintiff firms in the Washington, D.C., region are representing clients tied to last month’s collapse of the Francis Scott Key Bridge in Baltimore. And legal observers anticipate more lawyers to get involved as litigation and investigations proceed.

Blank Rome, Duane Morris, DiCello Levitt, Saltz Mongeluzzi Bendesky Trial Lawyers, Stewart Miller Simmons Trial Attorneys and Kreindler & Kreindler are among the firms that have been retained in matters related to the Baltimore bridge collapse, according to court records and media releases.
