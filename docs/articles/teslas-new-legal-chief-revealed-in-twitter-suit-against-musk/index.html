Tesla's New Legal Chief Revealed in Twitter Suit Against Musk

Law.com September 21, 2022
Elon MuskTwitter v. Musk

Tesla senior director of legal Dinna Eskin made it clear that she's now in charge of the EV maker's legal department, but her predecessor's status remains murky.

Tesla Inc. senior director of legal Dinna Eskin is acting as the electric vehicle maker’s general counsel, according to an affidavit she filed in connection with Twitter’s lawsuit to prevent Elon Musk from backing out of a deal to buy the social media company.
