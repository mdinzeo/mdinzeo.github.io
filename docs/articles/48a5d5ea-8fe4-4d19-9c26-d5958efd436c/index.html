Avantax CLO Gets $1.9 Million Golden Parachute After Cetera Merger

Law.com November 29, 2023

Avantax legal chief Tabitha Bailey will receive a golden parachute worth $1.9 million for giving up her post following the close of the wealth management firm’s merger with Cetera Holdings.

Bailey was promoted to chief legal officer in January, after Ann Bruder resigned following Avantax’s sale of its TaxAct software business and subsequent rebranding from Blucora that shifted the company’s focus from taxes to wealth management.
