Women IP Lawyers Say Winning More First-Chair Roles Hinges on Communicating Effectively, Not Swagger or a 'Lawyerly' Voice

Law.com August 26, 2024

In the world of intellectual property and patent litigation, trust between lawyers and their corporate clients is earned, not given. But it doesn’t come from winning cases. For in-house counsel, it’s cultivated well before a case goes to trial.

Be straightforward and realistic about the potential outcomes of a case. Own mistakes and don’t sugarcoat bad news. And, above all, keep the emails short and to the point.
