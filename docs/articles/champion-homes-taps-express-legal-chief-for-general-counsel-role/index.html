Champion Homes Taps Express Legal Chief for General Counsel Role

Law.com August 8, 2024

Modular home builder Champion Homes has found a new general counsel in Laurel Krueger, an in-house veteran who has spent three years as legal chief and corporate secretary at fashion retailer Express.

Krueger departed Express in June, two months after it filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and the same month it was acquired by an investment group that included shopping mall giant Simon Property Group. Champion announced her hiring in a securities filing this week.
