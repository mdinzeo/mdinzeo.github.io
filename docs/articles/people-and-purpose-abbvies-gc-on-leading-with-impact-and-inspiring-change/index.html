People and Purpose: AbbVie's GC on Leading With Impact and Inspiring Change

Law.com November 26, 2024

"People and purpose" drive Perry Siatis’ leadership approach. As executive vice president, general counsel, and secretary at pharmaceutical company AbbVie, Siatis emphasizes the importance of caring for his team and living by the company's mission to transform patients’ lives.

"It all comes down to caring for your people, being inclusive, trusting and inspiring them. When you do that, you create a team that's passionate about the company's mission," Siatis said.
