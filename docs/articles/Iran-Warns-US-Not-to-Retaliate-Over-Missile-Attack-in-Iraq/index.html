Iran Warns US Not to Retaliate Over Missile Attack in Iraq

Courthouse News Service January 7, 2020

(CN) – Responding to the killing of Iranian General Qassem Soleimani in a U.S. airstrike, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps fired “tens” of missiles at Ain Assad airbase in Iraq, which currently houses U.S. troops.

Another attack was aimed at a base in Irbil. The Pentagon confirmed the attacks in a statement late Tuesday.
