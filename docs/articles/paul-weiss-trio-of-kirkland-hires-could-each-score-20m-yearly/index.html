Paul Weiss’ Trio of Kirkland Hires Could Each Score $20M Yearly

Law.com August 24, 2023

Three of the Kirkland & Ellis partners heading to Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison could each earn $20 million in annual compensation at their new firm, according to people with knowledge of the partners’ moves.

Overall, annual pay for transactional partners Neel Sachdev and Roger Johnson in the U.K. and Eric Wedel in the U.S. could be more or less than $20 million, depending on how much client business they bring from Kirkland, their status in the firm’s share system, and Paul Weiss’ profitability, the people said.
