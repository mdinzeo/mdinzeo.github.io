'It Was the Next Graduation': How an In-House Lawyer Became a Serial Entrepreneur

Law.com April 4, 2024

Adam Nguyen said he is a problem solver who is “a little bit OCD by nature.” So when he sees things that are wrong, or things that are inefficient, he has an urge to make it right.

“That natural inclination to make things more efficient, to find a better way of doing things, is who I am,” he said. ”And that goes back to when I was in school and starting out as a young lawyer. I wanted more free time to hang out with my friends. I wanted to go to the gym. I wanted to do the best work for clients, but I wanted to do it in the best way possible. I wasn’t always just working harder.”
