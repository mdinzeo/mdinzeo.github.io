CLOC Legal Tech Rundown: Ironclad Custom AI, New Lexis Tool and More

Law.com May 9, 2024

ContractPodAi: On Monday, contract lifecycle management (CLM) provider ContractPodAi announced the general availability of Leah Helpdesk, its generative AI-powered application. The application will expand to provide legal support through Leah Draft—a generative AI-powered chatbot meant help structure and cleanly format legal documents. The expansion will automate contract drafting processes throughout the Leah tools. “In-house legal teams are increasingly taking on more work, and we’re excited that we can provide immediate relief to our customers, through Leah,” said Gabriel Buigas, EVP of legal and compliance solutions at Integreon. “With Leah’s new offerings and updates, customers can immediately take advantage, and reap the benefits, of GenAI services. These will enhance our ability to drive true transformation for our customers, at scale.”

Evisort + Icertis: On Tuesday, CLM and intelligence software provider Icertis announced a new alliance with CLM company Evisort. This strategic partnership platform will enable new and existing Icertis customers to leverage Evisort’s contract-specific AI engine, accelerating the ingestion and analysis of contract data to deliver insights and optimize workflows, according to the press release. Since Icertis works to transform the key contents of a contract into “strategic assets,” the ability to use software to extract the critical information contained in contracts, structuring it into a common data model, and connecting it with operational systems like ERP, SCM, CRM and HCM is key, the release stated. Indeed, Evisort’s contract-specific AI engine will work in concert with Icertis Discover Copilot to do just that. ”By integrating our contract-specific AI engine with the Icertis Contract Intelligence platform, we can enable joint customers to rapidly ingest and analyze both legacy and new agreements, accelerating insights that drive business performance. Our collaboration with Icertis will set new standards for how contracts are managed and executed, and deliver exceptional outcomes to our joint customers,” said Jerry Ting, CEO of Evisort, in the release.
