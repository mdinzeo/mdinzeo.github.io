Disney Reveals New Legal Chief's Pay, Consulting Agreement With Ex-General Counsel

Law.com February 10, 2022

Horacio Gutierrez will collect a $1.3 million base salary with the opportunity to earn millions more in bonuses and equity awards, while his predecessor, Alan Braverman, gets paid $40K a month for consulting services.

The Walt Disney Co. has disclosed the pay package for the media entertainment giant’s new general counsel along with details on the company’s consulting services agreement with its former legal chief.
