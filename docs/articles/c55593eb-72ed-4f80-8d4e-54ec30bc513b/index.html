Luxury Automaker Aston Martin, Buoyed by Resurgence, Hires First US-Based General Counsel

Law.com July 8, 2024

British sports carmaker Aston Martin has scooped up a Mercedes-Benz executive as its first U.S.-based general counsel—a move that reflects the increased importance of the American market for the company as it attempts to boost global sales from 6,000 to 10,000 vehicles per year.
