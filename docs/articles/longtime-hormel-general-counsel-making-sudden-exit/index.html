Longtime Hormel General Counsel Making Sudden Exit

Law.com September 28, 2023

Lori Marco, Hormel Foods’ general counsel for nearly 13 years, will depart at the end of October.

A Hormel spokesperson on Friday confirmed Marco’s planned departure and said the company is seeking a replacement, but declined to comment further. A day earlier, Hormel said in a U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filing that on Monday it “determined that … Marco … will separate from the company effective as of October 27, 2023.”
