What Dane Drobny Learned From Being GC of Groupon, Sears

Law.com May 14, 2023

Dane Drobny says that, when he was in law school, he thought being a lawyer was largely about advocacy and confrontation. He has since learned it’s more about problem-solving.

“People are coming to you, and they’re trying to solve business problems. They’re not really trying to solve legal problems,” he said. “They’re trying to solve business problems in a compliant way that manages any legal risk.”
