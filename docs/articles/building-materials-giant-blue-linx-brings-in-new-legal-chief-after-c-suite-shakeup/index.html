Building-Materials Giant Blue Linx Brings in New Legal Chief After C-Suite Shakeup

Law.com June 22, 2023

Wholesale building materials distributor Blue Linx has lured away ServiceMaster legal chief Tricia Kinney to serve as its chief legal officer.

The company recently underwent an executive shake-up that saw the sudden exit of CEO Dwight Gibson in March. Blue Linx disclosed that Gibson had left the company and vacated his board seat in a Securities and Exchange Commission filing, saying only that his departure would be treated as termination without cause. Gibson was paid a little more than $1.5 million in severance, according to the filing.
