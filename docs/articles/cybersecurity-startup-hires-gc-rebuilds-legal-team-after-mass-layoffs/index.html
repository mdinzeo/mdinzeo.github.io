Cybersecurity Startup Hires GC, Rebuilds Legal Team After Mass Layoffs

Law.com July 23, 2023
Cybersecurity

In-house veteran David Morris joined cybersecurity platform Snyk as general counsel as it continues to rebuild its legal team after a wave of layoffs last year that included its chief legal officer and head of legal operations.

Morris shared the news on LinkedIn. “Since 2015, Snyk has led the way for developer security to make the digital world a safer place. I’m proud to take part in their mission to help organizations worldwide develop applications securely,” he wrote.
