Ex-Moody's GC Gets 8 Months in Prison for Not Filing Tax Returns on $54M in Income

Law.com October 23, 2024
Tax LawCriminal Justice

John Goggins said in a statement that he "is grateful to the court for considering the comprehensive measures of accountability and restitution he has taken in connection with his misdemeanor offenses."

Former longtime Moody's general counsel John Goggins will serve eight months in prison for failing to file federal income taxes on more than $54 million he earned from 2018 through 2021.
