FTC Strikes Back at MGM for Fighting Its Probe of Massive Cyberattack

Law.com June 19, 2024
CybersecurityLina Khan (FTC Chair)

In one line of attack, MGM is seeking to disqualify FTC Chair Lina Khan, who was staying at the company's Las Vegas hotel when the hackers struck and, because of the disruption, was asked by a front desk clerk to write her credit card number on a piece of paper.

The Federal Trade Commission is asking a federal court to force MGM Resorts International to comply with its investigative demands regarding a crippling cyberattack in September that exposed the personal information of 1.5 million MGM guests and brought down the hotel and casino operator’s systems for more than a week.
