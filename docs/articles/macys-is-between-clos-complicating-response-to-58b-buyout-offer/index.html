Macy's Is Between CLOs, Complicating Response to $5.8B Buyout Offer

Law.com December 13, 2023

"It's always better to make a bid when things are confused," corporate governance expert Charles Elson said. "[And] it's harder to defend during a transitional period."

An investor group has sprung an unsolicited, $5.8 billion takeover bid on Macy’s at a highly awkward time for the department store giant—when it’s between chief legal officers.
