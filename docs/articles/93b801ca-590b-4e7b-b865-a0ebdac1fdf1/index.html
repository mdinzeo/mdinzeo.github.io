Retailer Chico's Names Ex-Ascena In-House Leader as General Counsel

Law.com September 19, 2021

“I am so excited to have joined this iconic multi-brand fashion retailer … that was founded by women, is led by women and whose mission is to be in service to women, providing solutions that bring millions of women confidence and joy!” Wendy Hufford stated.

Chico’s FAS Inc. has hired a former in-house leader for Ascena Retail Group and Boehringer Ingelheim as the fashion company’s general counsel, corporate secretary, chief compliance officer and senior vice president.
