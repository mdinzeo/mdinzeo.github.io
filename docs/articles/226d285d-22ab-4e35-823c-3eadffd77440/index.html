Law.com July 11, 2024

Welcome to Friday, You made it! If you feel worn to the bone, it’s perhaps because a fortuitous string of federal holidays has softened you up, eliminating one work day three of the last seven weeks. We better rebuild our stamina—the next holiday isn’t until Labor Day, Sept. 2. In today’s edition, we look at the ever-rising pay for the nation’s most elite law firm partners. Not long ago, $10 million was difficult to fathom. Now, the top range is $25 million to $30 million. Are the ever-higher payouts sustainable? Also, after going ga-ga for generative AI over the past two years, corporate America is experiencing something of a reality check. New research shows many companies say the results of their hefty AI investments are disappointing. We examine what went wrong.
