Gas Company Parts Ways With GC, Hires Visa Veteran as Successor

Law.com December 3, 2023

Natural gas company CNX Resources has hired veteran Visa attorney Timothy Bedard as general counsel, replacing longtime in-house lawyer Alexander Reyes.

Canonsburg, Pennsylvania-based CNX, which extracts natural gas from shale formations in the Appalachian Basin and elsewhere, said the company and Reyes mutually agreed to part ways. CNX says he is pursuing other career opportunities.
