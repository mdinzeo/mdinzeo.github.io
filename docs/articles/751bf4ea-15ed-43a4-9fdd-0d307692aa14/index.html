Jury Awards $6.1M to Former UC Riverside Faculty in Whistleblower Retaliation Verdict

Law.com June 30, 2024
Plaintiffs represented by Greene Broillet & Wheeler won $6.1 million in damages in a whistleblower retaliation trial against the UC Regents. Two former faculty members at UC Riverside's Department of Psychiatry and Neuroscience alleged that they were removed from their positions and effectively forced to resign after reporting the Department Chair's misuse of state funds, among other accusations. ...
