Porsche's Venture Capital Arm Adds General Counsel From Clifford Chance

Law.com November 20, 2024

Porsche Ventures, the Luxembourg-based venture capital arm of the luxury carmaker Porsche AG, has hired Clifford Chance lawyer Lynn Nussbaum as general counsel, according to a LinkedIn announcement from Nussbaum.

Nussbaum did not respond to a message from Law.com seeking comment on the move, and a Porsche spokesperson said the company "does not comment on personnel matters at this level." The role marks the first in-house job for the young lawyer, who joined Clifford Chance in 2020 after short stints as a legal intern at Luxembourg-based law firms Kleyr Grasso SCS, Trialys and Elvinger Hoss Prussen.
