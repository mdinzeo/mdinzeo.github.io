Outside Counsel Selection, Long Driven by Intuition, Being Reshaped by Data

Law.com April 25, 2024

Legal departments are starting to use metrics to determine whom to hire for outside legal work, instead of making those decisions based primarily on such criteria as trust, familiarity and a law firm’s reputation.

That was one of the central themes of a webinar last week hosted by the Buying Legal Council, an international trade association for in-house buyers of legal services and technology.
