US Judge Shuts Down French Court’s Picasso Copyright Ruling

Courthouse News Service September 12, 2019

(CN) – A French court’s €2 million judgment against a U.S. art editor who allegedly infringed on copyrighted photos of Pablo Picasso’s artworks will not stand, according to a federal judge who ruled them fair use on Thursday.

“The court is mindful of concerns over comity between the French and U.S. courts,” U.S. District Judge Edward Davila wrote in his ruling refusing to enforce the judgment. “However, the court finds that the defendants’ use of plaintiffs’ photographs qualifies as fair use.”
