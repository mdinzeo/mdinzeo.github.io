AARP In-House Veteran Tapped as 3E's GC

Law.com December 4, 2022

3E, a data and software company that helps companies handle hazardous materials and comply with environmental health and safety regulations, tapped in-house privacy veteran Audrey Jean as its general counsel.

“Happy to share that I’m starting a new position as General Counsel at 3E. Very excited to join this team!” Jean said in announcing her new position Monday on LinkedIn.
