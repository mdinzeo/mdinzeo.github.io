In-House Leaders Expected to Lead ESG Efforts, Stay Quiet About Social Issues, Report Says

Law.com May 17, 2022

Nearly all of the in-house lawyers who took part in a new survey said their legal departments were steering their companies’ ESG initiatives, but more than half said they weren't allowed to speak on social issues.

Companies are looking to their legal departments to lead environmental, social and governance strategies, but many businesses don’t want their in-house lawyers speaking publicly about social issues, such as racial justice and human rights.
