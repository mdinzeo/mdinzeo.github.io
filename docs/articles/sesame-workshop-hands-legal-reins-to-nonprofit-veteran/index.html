Sesame Workshop Hands Legal Reins to Nonprofit Veteran

Law.com May 16, 2023

Sesame Workshop, the nonprofit behind the long-running children’s educational television series “Sesame Street,” has tapped deputy general counsel Valerie Mitchell Johnston to lead its legal department.

Johnston will become general counsel and executive vice president of legal and business affairs on July 1, New York City-based Sesame Workshop announced Monday. She will take over from Joseph Salvo, who is retiring after eight years with the organization. He earned $496,376 in compensation in fiscal year 2020-2021, according to the organization’s latest tax filing.
