Kraft Heinz Legal Chief Quits, Effective Immediately

Law.com August 4, 2024

Kraft Heinz legal chief Rashida La Lande has resigned from her role effective immediately, the company disclosed Monday in a securities filing.

La Lande had been with the food and beverage giant since 2018, and was promoted to chief legal and corporate affairs officer at the end of last year.
