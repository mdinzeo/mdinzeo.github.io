Bill Would Unleash Hackers to Hunt for Federal Contractors' Cybersecurity Flaws

Law.com September 13, 2023
Cybersecurity

Federal contractors soon might have to adopt policies that allow ethical hackers to find and report cybersecurity vulnerabilities in their systems.

Vulnerability disclosure policies have been a requirement for federal agencies, along with smartphone, laptop and other device manufacturers and suppliers, since 2020.
