4 Longtime Netflix Legal Execs Out in Restructuring

Law.com October 4, 2023

Four attorneys who are senior members of Netflix’s legal and business affairs team are exiting as part of a restructuring, including 16-year company veteran Bryony Gagan, who oversaw global content production, acquisition and distribution.

Gagan has the title vice president of legal affairs, as do nine-year company veteran Amy Paquette and 13-year veteran Tim Mizrahi, who also are leaving. The fourth departure is nine-year veteran Stephen Zager, vice president and associate general counsel. The news was first reported by Deadline.
