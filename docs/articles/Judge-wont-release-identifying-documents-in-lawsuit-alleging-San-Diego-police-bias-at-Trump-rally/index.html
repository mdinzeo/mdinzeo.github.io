Judge won’t release identifying documents in lawsuit alleging San Diego police bias at Trump rally

Courthouse News Service October 18, 2021

A federal magistrate halted the release of documents that would reveal the identities of two suspected antifa members as well as one pro-Trump demonstrator who allegedly lobbed a smoke grenade at counter-protesters at a Jan. 9 rally in San Diego.

SAN DIEGO (CN) — The city of San Diego does not have to disclose the identity of a suspect who allegedly threw an incendiary device at anti-Trump demonstrators at a Pacific Beach “Patriot March” on Jan. 9 where Trump supporters clashed with counter-protesters.
