9th Circuit Clarifies When Online Marketplaces Are Liable for User-Generated Content

Law.com July 23, 2023
An appeals court found that online marketplaces are liable for third-party content when they have specific knowledge of infringers or infringement. The court found removing infringing listings might be sufficient to show that an online marketplace was not willfully blind to infringement.
