Attorneys for Ex-Twitter CLO Call Out Elon Musk in Billing Dispute

Law.com August 16, 2023

Lawyers for ex-Twitter CLO Vijaya Gadde blasted the platform and its new owner for failing "Although Twitter would like to pretend it is a party that dutifully pays its contractual obligations as they come due, it is in fact perpetually delinquent and is gaining a well-earned reputation for shirking its commitments,"

In the latest in the ongoing saga over former Twitter legal chief Vijaya Gadde’s unpaid legal bills, her attorneys hit back at the social media platform—and its new owner—for refusing to pony up $1.1 million for fees she incurred in preparing to testify before Congress earlier this year, saying in a court filing this week that Twitter “just does not want to pay.”
