'We’re Here to Empower People to Make Good Decisions': Why Compliance Chiefs Must Learn to Think Like a Businessperson

Law.com November 18, 2024

General counsel and chief compliance officers must build relationships across the company when times are good to earn the trust they'll need to navigate the business through future compliance and ethical challenges.

That was a theme of Monday's opening keynote at ALM Global's two-day General Counsel Conference East at the Marriott Marquis in Midtown Manhattan. Discussing the topic with ALM's Heather Nevitt were Visa Chief Ethics and Compliance Officer Obiamaka Madubuko and Front-Line Anti-Bribery CEO Richard Bistrong.
