California enforcement official gives view to AG's privacy enforcement approach

MLex February 6, 2025

silence doesn’t mean inactivity, according to Stacey Schesser, supervising deputy attorney general for privacy and consumer protection at the California Department of Justice. The state does use publicly-announced sweep letters to keep companies on their toes, and is increasingly coordinating with other states and global regulators. She also warned companies of an alarming trend - that e-mails sent to privacy inboxes are going ignored.
