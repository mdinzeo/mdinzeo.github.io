Which M&A Attorneys Drove the Most Business as Deal Leads?

Law.com February 15, 2024

Who are the lead rainmakers of M&A in Big Law? A look at the top law firm partners based on the value of their deals shows the typical crowd of New York elites—along with a couple others who have edged out competitors in recent years to enter the top tier of M&A work.

Mergerlinks, a financial data provider, generated the rankings based on total deal value of the deals they led. With the caveat that those who drive a deal are known really only to the deal teams, there are seven different dealmaker firms on the chart, with Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz taking three of those spots and Sullivan & Cromwell taking two.
