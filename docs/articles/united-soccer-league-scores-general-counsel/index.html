United Soccer League Scores General Counsel

Law.com November 26, 2024

The United Soccer League has hired Tampa, Florida, attorney Andrew McIntosh as its executive vice president and general counsel, the USL announced.

McIntosh is a business and transactions lawyer at Shumaker, Loop & Kendrick, where he will stay as of counsel and has worked for nearly a dozen years on matters including corporate governance and shareholder disputes.
