Female GCs Who Break Into Fortune 1000 Earn Big Bucks, But Men Hold Most of the Jobs

Law.com September 5, 2023
Employment Law

Female general counsel at Fortune 1000 companies received slightly higher total compensation than their male counterparts last year, but they continued to be drastically outnumbered.

That was among the key findings of Corporate Counsel’s analysis of female representation on its 2023 list of highest-paid general counsel. The list, created in partnership with ALM Intelligence, was based on U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission pay disclosures of the Fortune 1000 for fiscal 2022.
