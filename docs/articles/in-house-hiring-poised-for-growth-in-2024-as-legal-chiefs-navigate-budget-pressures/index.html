In-House Hiring Poised for Growth in 2024 as Legal Chiefs Navigate Budget Pressures

Law.com January 4, 2024

Legal industry analysts and some general counsel are predicting a robust in-house hiring market for 2024, even amid budgetary constraints by some corporate counsel.

Specialized attorney roles within corporate legal departments, such as those focused on data privacy, legal ops and artificial intelligence, are particularly in demand, according to interviews with general counsel and others.
