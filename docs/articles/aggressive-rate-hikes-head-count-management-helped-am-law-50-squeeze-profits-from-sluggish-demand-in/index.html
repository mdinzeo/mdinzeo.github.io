Aggressive Rate Hikes, Head Count Management Helped Am Law 50 Squeeze Profits From Sluggish Demand in Q4

Law.com February 12, 2024
The Am Law 50 posted strong gains in profitability in Q4 despite slow demand, thanks to headcount management and rate hikes. Worked rates went up 6.5% across a cohort of Am Law 200 and midsize firms in Thomson Reuters’ Q4 2023 Law Firm Financial Index, while demand rose 1.7%. Direct expenses rose 5.3% in Q4 compared to Q4 2022 while overhead expenses went up 6.6%. ...
