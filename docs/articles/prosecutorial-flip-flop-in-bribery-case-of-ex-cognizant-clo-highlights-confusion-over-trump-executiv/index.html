Prosecutorial Flip-Flop in Bribery Case of Ex-Cognizant CLO Highlights Confusion Over Trump Executive Order

Law.com March 5, 2025
Criminal Justice

Confusion over the implications of President Donald Trump’s executive order pausing Foreign Corrupt Practices Act enforcement is so thick that even officials within the Justice Department disagree on what it means.

That became apparent Tuesday after the newly installed U.S. attorney for the District of New Jersey asked a judge to delay the bribery trial for former Cognizant Chief Legal Officer Steven Schwartz and former Cognizant President Gordon Coburn for 180 days to give his office time to assess how Trump’s Feb. 10 FCPA order applies to the case. Jury selection had been set to begin this week.
