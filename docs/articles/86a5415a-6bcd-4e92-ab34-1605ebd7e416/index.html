HP Legal Chief's 2023 Pay Included $2M Cash Bonus for Leaving Yahoo

Law.com February 26, 2024

HP Chief Legal Officer Julie Jacobs received $6.2 million in compensation in her first full year on the job, a sum that includes a $2 million cash bonus.

HP explains in its newly filed proxy statement that the bonus, paid out in fiscal year 2023, represents a one-time payment “in recognition of foregone compensation at her previous employer.”
