Cleared in HP Fraud Trial, British Tech Tycoon Mike Lynch Now Missing at Sea

Law.com August 19, 2024
Autonomy v. HP

British tech entrepreneur Mike Lynch is lost at sea after a luxury yacht sank during a sudden thunderstorm off the coast of Sicily.

Lynch, 59, is among six people who are missing. His wife, Angela Bacares, is one of 15 who were rescued. News reports say Bacares is the owner of the yacht “Bayesian,” which was carrying a total of 22 people—12 passengers and 10 crew members—when it ran into a bout of bad weather in the early morning hours on Monday.
