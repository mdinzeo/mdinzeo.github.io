Bumble Finds Successor to Legal Chief Who Moved On in Its Own Hive

Law.com June 17, 2024

After five months of Elizabeth Monteleone’s serving as Bumble’s chief legal officer on an interim basis, the company has given her the job permanently.

Monteleone has been with the Austin, Texas-based online dating and networking app since 2018, when she met one of its earliest employees on its Bumble For Friends site. It was her first in-house role; Monteleone was previously an associate with Winstead in Austin and an associate with Proskauer Rose in New York.
