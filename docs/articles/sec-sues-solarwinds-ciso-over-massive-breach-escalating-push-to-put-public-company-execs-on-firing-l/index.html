SEC Sues SolarWinds CISO Over Massive Breach, Escalating Push to Put Public Company Execs on Firing Line

Law.com October 30, 2023
Cybersecurity
The Securities and Exchange Commission accused SolarWinds and its security chief, Tim Brown, of fraud and internal controls violations. It was the agency's first enforcement action against a computer-security executive over a hack. Solarwinds called the allegations unfounded, and an attorney for Brown said he has performed his job with distinction and has continuously improved the company's cybersecurity. ...
