High-Flying Genetics Testing Firm GeneDx Hires Ex-Zoetis GC as Legal Chief

Law.com November 19, 2024

The former general counsel of Pfizer's animal health spinoff Zoetis has been named chief legal officer of GeneDx, a genetic testing company whose rare-disease datasets are used to tailor precise medical treatments for pediatric patients.

Stamford, Connecticut-based GeneDx announced on Monday that Heidi Chen will head legal and compliance functions, including regulatory matters and intellectual property.
