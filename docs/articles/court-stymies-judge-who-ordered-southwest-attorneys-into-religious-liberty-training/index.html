Court Stymies Judge Who Ordered Southwest Attorneys Into 'Religious Liberty' Training

Law.com June 10, 2024

The Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals has spared, at least for now, Southwest Airlines from having to send a trio of its attorneys to “religious liberty” training, ruling that a judge who ordered the training as part of civil-contempt proceedings last summer likely far exceeded his authority.
