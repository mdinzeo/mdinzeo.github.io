Ex-Twitter Exec Sues for $20M, Says Musk Fired Her as 'Petty Retribution'

Law.com November 4, 2024
Elon MuskEmployment Law

Another former Twitter executive has joined the chorus of fired employees suing Elon Musk alleging unpaid severance. This time, it's ex-chief marketing officer Leslie Berland, who says she was fired so that Musk could avoid paying her $20 million in severance and benefits.

Berland, who until October 2022 was Twitter's chief marketing officer, said Musk fired her in an act of "petty retribution" after a meeting with advertisers went off the rails, and also to "rid himself of her large compensation package."
