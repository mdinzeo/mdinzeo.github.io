Legal Chief of Retailer Beyond Exiting at Tumultuous Time

Law.com November 24, 2024

Beyond Inc., the housewares giant formerly known as Overstock, is losing its legal chief on Dec. 31, according to a late Friday securities filing.

E. Glen Nickle joined the company as associate general counsel in 2016 and became chief legal officer in 2021.
