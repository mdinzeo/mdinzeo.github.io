Female GCs Say Too Many Women Run Legal Departments Without Getting the Title

Law.com October 10, 2022
Employment Law

Women looking to claim leadership of their legal departments often must advocate strongly for themselves to overcome workplace biases, panelists at a webinar last week agreed.

Sometimes, women end up with the responsibilities of running the department but without the title, the panelists said.
