Portland Trail Blazers GC Returns to Big Law

Law.com February 13, 2023

After 13 years with the Portland Trail Blazers, longtime legal chief Ben Lauritsen is rejoining Stoel Rives as partner in its corporate practice group.

After a two-year stint in the London office of Latham & Watkins, Lauritsen was an associate at Portland-based Stoel Rives from 2008-2010, where he advised clients on corporate financing, restructuring, mergers and acquisitions, and bankruptcy. In a statement, he said he remained close with his former Stoel Rives colleagues, so his return “really feels like a homecoming.”
